Q: In the figure, l ∥ m. A path crosses l, bends between the two lines and then crosses m. Find the angle marked ? where the path meets the lower line m.

Tip: Draw a line through the bend parallel to l and m. It splits the bend angle into an upper part and a lower part. Alternate angles are equal; co-interior angles add up to 180°.

Give the angle in degrees.

Answer: 40°

Explanation: Through the bend draw a line parallel to l and m. The 100° angle and the upper part of the bend angle are co-interior angles (between the parallel lines, on the same side of the path), so the upper part is 180° − 100° = 80°. The bend angle is 120°, so the lower part is 120° − 80° = 40°. The lower part and the bottom angle are alternate angles, so the bottom angle is 40°.
